2. What Text Scanner does

Text Scanner is an offline Android app that recognizes text in images, groups multiple pages into documents, lets you edit and organize the results, reads text using installed offline voices, and exports or shares text and documents. Supported recognition scripts are Latin, Chinese, Devanagari, Japanese, and Korean.

Features depend on your device, Android services, image quality, available storage, and any installed text-to-speech voices.

3. Review recognized text carefully

OCR results can be wrong or incomplete.

Before relying on, sending, or publishing recognized text, compare it with the original image and correct any errors. Do not treat a scan as a verified copy of a source document.

Blur, glare, handwriting, unusual fonts, low resolution, page damage, mixed scripts, and a language selection that does not match the image can affect recognition. Page order, punctuation, numbers, names, and formatting may also need correction.
