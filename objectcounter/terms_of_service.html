2. Description of the Service

Object Counter is a mobile app that uses on-device computer-vision models to estimate the number and location of objects visible through a live camera or in a captured or imported image.

The app offers two counting methods. Recognize attempts to detect and name familiar object classes. Shape Count is intended for repeated, angled, or tightly packed items and may organize detections into visual groups. The app can annotate still images and store selected results locally on your device.
